The platform

Fly, update, and intervene from a single dashboard.

OneField brings the full lifecycle of a drone mission into one place, from firmware to flight plan to live control. Your operators don't need to be on site.

Smart software management

Every aircraft in the fleet runs the same firmware, and you never have to touch a cable to keep it that way.

  • Over-the-air updatesDeploy firmware and software patches remotely and securely.
  • Version controlSchedule updates across the fleet, or roll back when you need to.

Pre-programmed flight paths

Draw a route once and fly it the same way every time.

  • Mission planningDesign complex paths on a drag-and-drop map, or import KML/GeoJSON.
  • Environmental awarenessOptimize routes with real-time weather and terrain data.
  • Repeatable missionsSave and reuse plans for consistent data collection.

Remote override & live control

Watch every flight as it happens, and take over the moment something goes wrong.

  • Real-time interventionTake manual control of any drone during an emergency or when it meets an obstacle.
  • Live video feedCamera feeds and telemetry on one centralized dashboard.
  • Fail-safe protocolsOverride safely without compromising mission integrity.

How it works

From sign-up to first flight in five steps.

This is the DJI workflow from our quick start guide. Connect several drones at once with a CSV batch import or QR sync.

  1. Create your account

    Sign up and choose your organization type: Agriculture, Energy, or Public Safety.

  2. Connect your drones

    Under Fleet › Add Drone, enter your DJI Cloud API credentials or scan the QR code from DJI Pilot.

  3. Plan the mission

    Draw a path or upload a file, then set altitude, speed, and camera actions.

  4. Enable remote access

    Turn on Remote Override, decide who can take control, and enable OTA updates.

  5. Monitor & control

    Use Live View and the Command Console to pause, override, or trigger return-to-home.

Use cases

Built for teams that fly where people can't easily go.

Agriculture

Crop health & irrigation

Automated flyovers on a schedule show crop health and irrigation patterns, and every survey covers the same ground the same way.

Energy & utilities

Remote inspections

Inspect power lines, wind turbines, and pipelines without sending crews out to the line. Live video lets an operator spot a fault and review it on the spot.

Public safety

Coordinate several aircraft over a search area with real-time situational awareness, and step in manually when you need to.
